About

Seoryeon Lee is a humanities activist who shares reading, meditation and happiness. She completed doctoral coursework in Buddhist Philosophy at Dongguk University. As a researcher on the Buddhist Records and Cultural Heritage Archive (ABC) project at Dongguk's Institute of Buddhist Studies and at Dongguk's Manhae Research Institute, she worked with old records, then turned to the public humanities, hoping that a spark in one person's heart might become a lantern of their own.

She was there at the beginning of Jigwanseoga as CEO of Seedrise. After regional research, interviews with citizens, finding idle municipal spaces and curating the first books, she opened a reading group in 2021 where citizens share the books of their lives, as curator of the Jigwanseoga at Ulsan Grand Park. She then took charge of Jigwanseoga and the humanities outreach projects at the Plato Academy Foundation (now the Jigwan Foundation, 止觀), planning and leading lectures, reading groups and humanities activities across 11 Jigwanseoga spaces in Ulsan, Yeoju, Andong, Uljin, Suwon and Pyeongtaek. The results include more than 100 humanities lectures, the 55-session reading group "Evening of Reading", a reading challenge now in its 7th season, 263 issues of the newsletter "Weekly Jigwan", national forums, and a book festival themed "A Day Where Reading, Meditation and Happiness Flow".

Bringing the humanities beyond the page was also her work. She planned the SBS Biz humanities lecture program "Life, Stopping to Look" and the CJ ENM Sapiens Studio original series "Discovery of Life" (Plato Academy x Sapiens Studio), and she plans the content of the two YouTube channels of the Jigwan Foundation and Jigwanseoga, often holding the camera herself. As an instructor of Heart-Smile Training (HST), a meditation researched at the KAIST Meditation Research Institute, she shares gatherings where reading meets mindfulness.

She received the 2024 Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism Commendation for contributing to the social spread of humanistic culture. The Jigwan Foundation, where she works, received the 2026 Prime Minister's Commendation at the 32nd Reading Culture Awards for creating Jigwanseoga. She is currently Principal Researcher at the Jigwan Foundation.
